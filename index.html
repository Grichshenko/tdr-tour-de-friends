Tour De Friends is a Norwegian cycling community that welcomes young adults who are passionate about cycling, exploring new destinations, and connecting with like-minded individuals.

Our
Mission

Tour De Friends aims to create a community of young adults who are passionate about cycling, exploring new destinations, and connecting with like-minded individuals.

We provide a platform for our members to form meaningful relationships and create unforgettable memories while exploring the world on two wheels.

Our Values

Tour De Friends values inclusivity, friendship, and adventure. We strive to create a welcoming and inclusive community where everyone feels valued and appreciated.

We believe that cycling is a way to connect with nature, discover new destinations, and challenge oneself both physically and mentally.

Our Equipment

Tour De Friends provides high-quality equipment to ensure that everyone can enjoy the thrill of cycling. We offer a range of bicycles, including road bikes, mountain bikes, and hybrid bikes, to suit different preferences and abilities.

Additionally, we provide helmets, locks, and other necessary gear to ensure that everyone is safe and comfortable during their ride.
